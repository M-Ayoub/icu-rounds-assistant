VENTILATOR PARAMETERS

Enter the ventilator and patient measurements, then calculate.

Reference values are clinical guides, not automatic ventilator prescriptions. Interpret alongside diagnosis, mechanics, gas exchange and clinical condition.

Use measured height where possible. For driving pressure, use total PEEP, including intrinsic PEEP when present. Static compliance assumes a valid plateau pressure measurement during passive conditions.

🧪ABG analyzer

ACID–BASE AND OXYGENATION

Enter ABG values to calculate acid–base parameters.

Assumes an arterial sample and standard units. The analyzer provides a preliminary pattern, not a definitive diagnosis. Check the clinical context, sample validity, compensation, lactate, ketones and other causes of mixed disorders.
